# UC Santa Cruz — Computer Science B.S. Prerequisites & Requirements
> Source: UCSC Baskin School of Engineering, 2025-2026 General Catalog

## Major Qualification (Declaration) Requirements

To declare the CS B.S. major, you must complete these screening courses with a **minimum 3.0 GPA**:

| Course | Title | Units | Notes |
|--------|-------|-------|-------|
| MATH 19A (or 20A) | Calculus for Science, Engineering, and Mathematics | 5 | Must be letter grade |
| MATH 19B (or 20B) | Calculus for Science, Engineering, and Mathematics | 5 | Must be letter grade |
| CSE 30 | Programming Abstractions: Python | 7 | Must be letter grade |
| CSE 12 | Computer Systems and Assembly Language + Lab | 7 | Must be letter grade |
| CSE 13S | Computer Systems and C Programming | 7 | Must be letter grade |

**Rules:**
- No more than one non-passing grade (below C) in qualification courses
- Must declare major by 6th quarter at UCSC
- CS is an impacted major — if not admitted as CS, switching in is extremely limited

## Elective Groups

Students must complete 5 upper-division elective courses from approved lists.

### Disciplinary Communication (DC) — Choose 1:
- CSE 115A: Introduction to Software Engineering (CSE 101)
- CSE 185S: Technical Writing for Computer Science/Engineering

### Capstone — Choose 1:
- CSE 115B: Software Design Project II
- CSE 195: Senior Thesis

### Electives — Choose 3 from:
- CSE 110A: Compiler Design (CSE 101, CSE 114A)
- CSE 111: Database Systems (CSE 101)
- CSE 115C: Software Design Project III
- CSE 118: Mobile Applications (CSE 101)
- CSE 140: Artificial Intelligence (CSE 101)
- CSE 142: Machine Learning (CSE 101, CSE 107)
- CSE 144: Applied Machine Learning (CSE 101)
- CSE 150: Introduction to Computer Networks (CSE 130)
- CSE 156: Network Programming (CSE 130)
- CSE 160: Introduction to Computer Graphics (CSE 101, MATH 23A)
- CSE 163: Data Programming for Visualization (CSE 101)
- CSE 180: Database Systems II (CSE 111)
- CSE 183: Web Applications (CSE 101)

## Total Units Required: ~180 quarter units

## Recommended 4-Year Plan

### Year 1 (Freshman)
- **Fall:** CSE 30, MATH 19A
- **Winter:** CSE 12, MATH 19B
- **Spring:** CSE 13S, CSE 16

### Year 2 (Sophomore)
- **Fall:** CSE 100, CSE 101, MATH 23A
- **Winter:** CSE 102, CSE 107
- **Spring:** CSE 103, CSE 114A

### Year 3 (Junior)
- **Fall:** CSE 120, Elective 1
- **Winter:** CSE 130, Elective 2
- **Spring:** CSE 115A (DC), Elective 3

### Year 4 (Senior)
- **Fall:** Elective 4, Elective 5
- **Winter:** Capstone
- **Spring:** Capstone/Thesis completion
