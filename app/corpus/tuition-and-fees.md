## Financial Aid

- **61-71% of undergraduates** receive financial aid
- **FAFSA deadline:** March 2 (priority)
- **California Dream Act Application** available for AB 540 eligible students
- **UC Blue and Gold Plan:** Families with income under $80,000/year → tuition and fees fully covered by grants/scholarships

### Common Aid Types

| Aid Type | Description | Amount Range |
|----------|-------------|-------------|
| Pell Grant | Federal need-based | Up to $7,395/yr |
| Cal Grant A | CA residents, need + merit | Up to $12,570/yr |
| Cal Grant B | CA residents, high need | Up to $1,648 + tuition |
| UC Grant | Institutional need-based | Varies |
| Regents Scholarship | UC merit-based | $2,500-$10,000/yr |
| Federal Work-Study | Part-time employment | $2,500-$3,000/yr |
| Federal Direct Loans | Subsidized/Unsubsidized | $5,500-$7,500/yr |

### UC Tuition Stability Plan
Once enrolled, tuition remains stable and predictable — your cohort tuition is locked for up to 6 years.

## Payment Options
- **BARC (Billing and Receivables):** Pay via MyUCSC portal
- **Installment Plan:** Divide quarterly charges into 3 monthly payments ($50 enrollment fee)
- **Third-party Payments:** Sponsor authorization via MyUCSC
