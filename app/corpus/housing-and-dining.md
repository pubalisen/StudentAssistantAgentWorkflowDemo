## Housing Options & Rates (2025-2026)

### Residence Halls
Required for most first-year students. Shared rooms with communal bathrooms. Meal plan required.

| Room Type | Per Quarter | Per Year (3 quarters) |
|-----------|-------------|----------------------|
| Triple (standard) | $4,131 | $12,393 |
| Quad | $4,173 | $12,519 |
| Double | $5,250 | $15,750 |
| Single (limited) | $6,100 | $18,300 |

### Apartments
Available for continuing students. Include kitchens. Meal plan optional.

| Room Type | Per Quarter | Per Year (3 quarters) |
|-----------|-------------|----------------------|
| Quad bedroom | $4,416 | $13,248 |
| Triple bedroom | $4,329 | $12,987 |
| Double bedroom | $5,697 | $17,091 |
| Single bedroom | $6,480 | $19,440 |

### Family Student Housing
- **Laureate Court** and **Cardiff Terrace**
- 1-bedroom: ~$1,500/month
- 2-bedroom: ~$1,800/month

## Meal Plans

### Required Plans (Residence Hall Students)

| Plan | Per Quarter | Description |
|------|-------------|-------------|
| **Unlimited 7-Day** | ~$3,450 | All-you-can-eat access, 7 days/week |
| **Unlimited 5-Day** | ~$3,150 | All-you-can-eat, Mon-Fri |
| **125 Meals** | ~$3,150 | 125 swipes per quarter + Slug Points |
| **100 Meals** | ~$2,650 | 100 swipes per quarter + Slug Points |

### Voluntary Plans (Apartment/Off-Campus Students)

| Plan | Per Quarter | Description |
|------|-------------|-------------|
| **55 Meals** | ~$1,850 | 55 swipes + Slug Points |
| **Slug Points Only** | Varies | Dining credits ($1 = 1 point) |

### Slug Points
- Function like dining dollars
- Accepted at all campus dining locations: dining halls, cafés, markets
- Managed via the **GET app** on your phone
- Unused points roll over within the academic year (not across years)

## Housing Application Timeline

| Deadline | Event |
|----------|-------|
| **May 1** | New student housing application deadline (guaranteed space) |
| **May 15** | Continuing student housing lottery opens |
| **June 15** | Room assignments begin |
| **July 1** | Housing contract and meal plan selections due |
| **Aug 1** | Roommate matching results released |
| **Sept 15-20** | Move-in weekend (varies by college) |

## Important Policies
- **Housing guarantee:** First-year students who apply by May 1 are guaranteed a space
- **2-year residency requirement:** UC Santa Cruz recommends (but doesn't require) 2 years on campus
- **Break housing:** Residence halls close during winter and spring breaks; apartments remain open
- **Service animals & ESAs:** Contact Disability Resource Center for accommodation requests
- **Quiet hours:** 10 PM - 8 AM (24-hour quiet hours during finals week)
