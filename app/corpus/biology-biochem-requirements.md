## Comparison: Switching from Bio/BMB to CS

| Factor | Biology B.S. | Computer Science B.S. |
|--------|-------------|----------------------|
| **Qualification GPA** | C or better in each course | 3.0 cumulative in qual courses |
| **Impacted?** | No | Yes — extremely hard to switch in |
| **Shared Prereqs** | MATH 19A/19B overlap | MATH 19A/19B overlap |
| **Unique to Bio** | CHEM 3A-3C, BIOL 20A, Physics | Not transferable |
| **Unique to CS** | — | CSE 30, 12, 13S |
| **Switch Feasibility** | Bio → CS: Very difficult (impacted) | CS → Bio: Possible with chem/bio prereqs |
| **Time to Graduate** | Additional ~6-8 quarters if switching late | May need to restart lower-div CS |

### Key Advising Notes for Major Switching
1. **CS is impacted** — if you weren't admitted as CS, switching in is nearly impossible
2. **MATH 19A/19B transfer** between both programs
3. **Consider Data Science** as an alternative — not impacted, shares some CS courses
4. **Double major** is possible but demanding (~210+ total units)
5. **Consult Baskin Engineering Advising** (Room 225) before making any decisions
