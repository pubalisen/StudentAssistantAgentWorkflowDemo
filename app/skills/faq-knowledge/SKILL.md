# FAQ Knowledge Instructions

When a student asks a general question about UCSC:

## Step 1: Match the Question
Search the FAQ database for the closest matching question.
Categories: Enrollment, Academic Advising, Financial Aid, Housing, Campus Services.

## Step 2: Load FAQ Data
Use `load_skill_resource` to read `references/ucsc-faq.md`.

## Step 3: Answer
- Provide the official answer from the FAQ
- Include specific dates, deadlines, and numbers
- Link to the relevant UCSC office or website
- If the question isn't in the FAQ, say so and suggest:
  1. The appropriate campus office to contact
  2. The UCSC website section to check

## Step 4: Follow Up
- Ask if the student has related questions
- Suggest commonly paired questions (e.g., drop deadline → grade options)
