# UC Santa Cruz — Campus Map & Building Directory
> Source: UCSC Maps & Transportation, 2025-2026

## Campus Overview
UC Santa Cruz sits on 2,001 acres of redwood forest overlooking Monterey Bay. The campus is built into a hillside, with buildings organized into clusters connected by roads, paths, and shuttle routes.

**Main Entrance:** Bay Street and High Street intersection
**GPS Address:** 1156 High Street, Santa Cruz, CA 95064

## Campus Transit

### UCSC Shuttles (Free with Slug Card)
| Route | Loop | Frequency |
|-------|------|-----------|
| **Inner Loop** | Clockwise through campus core | Every 10-15 min |
| **Outer Loop** | Counter-clockwise, wider coverage | Every 10-15 min |
| **Night Owl** | Limited stops after 7 PM | Every 20 min |
| **Upper Campus** | Science Hill ↔ Central Campus | Every 15 min |

### Santa Cruz Metro (Free with Slug Card)
- **Route 10** — UCSC ↔ Downtown Santa Cruz ↔ Beach
- **Route 15** — UCSC ↔ West Side
- **Route 16** — UCSC ↔ Downtown (express)
- **Route 19** — UCSC ↔ East Santa Cruz ↔ Capitola
