# UC Santa Cruz — Frequently Asked Questions
> Source: Compiled from UCSC Registrar, Advising, and Student Services

## Enrollment & Registration

### Q: When is the deadline to drop a class without a "W" on my transcript?
**A:** The deadline to drop without a W varies by quarter:
- Fall 2025: October 24, 2025
- Winter 2026: January 30, 2026
- Spring 2026: April 24, 2026
After this date, dropping shows a "W" (Withdrawal) on your transcript. The W deadline is approximately 3 weeks later.

### Q: How does the two-pass enrollment system work?
**A:** UCSC enrollment has 3 passes:
- **Pass 1:** You can enroll in up to half your planned units. Priority is based on class standing (seniors first).
- **Pass 2:** Fill remaining units up to your max.
- **Pass 3 (Open Enrollment):** First-come, first-served for any remaining seats.
Check your appointment time in MyUCSC Student Center.

### Q: What is the maximum number of units I can take per quarter?
**A:** The standard maximum is 19 units per quarter. Students with a 3.0+ GPA can petition for up to 22 units. First-year students are limited to 15 units in their first quarter.

### Q: Can I take a class Pass/No Pass instead of for a letter grade?
**A:** Yes, but with restrictions:
- Major requirements must be taken for a letter grade
- GE requirements can be taken P/NP
- You must change grading option by the deadline (approximately week 7)
- P = C or better; NP = below C (no units earned)

### Q: How do I get a transcript?
**A:** Order through MyUCSC → Student Center → Academics → Transcript. Official transcripts are $15 each (electronic delivery). Unofficial transcripts are free and available immediately.

## Academic Advising

### Q: How do I declare my major?
**A:** Process varies by school:
1. Complete all major qualification courses
2. Meet the required GPA for your major
3. Submit a major declaration form through your department
4. For impacted majors (CS, Data Science), you must have been admitted to the major

### Q: What happens if I fail a major qualification course?
**A:** You can retake it once. Both grades appear on your transcript, but only the second attempt counts in your GPA. If you fail twice, you may need to petition or consider an alternative major.

### Q: Can I switch majors after my first year?
**A:** For non-impacted majors, yes — complete the new major's qualification requirements and submit paperwork. For impacted majors (Computer Science, Data Science, Computer Engineering), switching is extremely limited and often not possible.

### Q: What is academic probation?
**A:** You are placed on probation if your cumulative GPA falls below 2.0 or your quarterly GPA is below 1.5. You have one quarter to bring your GPA above 2.0, or you may face subject to dismissal (SSD) status.

## Financial Aid & Billing

### Q: When is the FAFSA deadline?
**A:** The priority deadline is **March 2** each year. You can submit after this date but may receive less aid. Use the FAFSA (Federal) or California Dream Act Application.

### Q: What is the UC Blue and Gold Opportunity Plan?
**A:** If your family's annual income is below $80,000/year, UC covers your systemwide tuition and fees through grants and scholarships (no loans required).

### Q: How does the installment payment plan work?
**A:** You can split your quarterly charges into 3 monthly payments. There's a $50 enrollment fee per quarter. Sign up through MyUCSC BARC account.

### Q: I didn't get enough financial aid. What can I do?
**A:** Options:
1. **Appeal:** Submit a financial aid appeal if your circumstances changed
2. **Scholarships:** Apply via the UCSC scholarship portal (one application covers 100+ scholarships)
3. **Work-Study:** Accept Federal Work-Study in your aid package
4. **Emergency Funds:** Apply through the Slug Support program for unexpected expenses

## Housing & Campus Life

### Q: Is on-campus housing guaranteed?
**A:** Housing is guaranteed for first-year students who submit applications by the deadline (typically May 1). Transfer and continuing students are not guaranteed housing but can apply via the housing lottery.

### Q: What are the 10 residential colleges?
**A:** Each college has its own culture, GE requirements, and community:
1. Cowell — oldest, social justice focus
2. Stevenson — self-reliance, global awareness
3. Crown — science and engineering community
4. Merrill — cultural diversity, ethics
5. Porter — arts and creative expression
6. Kresge — environmental stewardship
7. Oakes — diversity and social change
8. Rachel Carson — environmental studies and sustainability
9. College Nine — international, global perspective
10. John R. Lewis — social justice and equity

### Q: What meal plan options are available?
**A:** All on-campus residents have a mandatory meal plan:
- **55 Meals/Quarter:** ~$1,850 (commuter-friendly)
- **100 Meals/Quarter:** ~$2,650 (standard)
- **125 Meals/Quarter:** ~$3,150 (all-access)
- **Unlimited:** ~$3,450

## Campus Services

### Q: Where is the Student Health Center?
**A:** Located at the Cowell Student Health Center. Services include primary care, mental health counseling (CAPS), immunizations, and pharmacy. Most services are covered by the Student Health Fee.

### Q: How do I get a campus ID (Slug Card)?
**A:** Visit the Slug Card Office in the Bay Tree Building, 2nd floor. Bring a photo ID. Your Slug Card provides building access, meal plan, library privileges, and transit bus pass.

### Q: Is there free transportation?
**A:** Yes! Your Slug Card includes unlimited rides on Santa Cruz Metro buses. UCSC also operates free campus shuttle loops that run every 10-15 minutes during the academic year.
